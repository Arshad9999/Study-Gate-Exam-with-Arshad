01 / CONCEPTUAL FOUNDATION

Start with the fundamentals

A percentage is a fraction out of 100. A 20% increase on 100 gives 120; a subsequent 20% decrease gives 96, because the base changed.

03 / FOUNDATIONAL TERMINOLOGY & TAXONOMY

Core Definitions & Classifications

Standardized academic definitions and structural classifications essential for conceptual questions:

Harmonic Mean Average Speed Quantitative Motion

When equal distances are traversed at speeds v_1 and v_2, Average Speed = 2 v_1 v_2 / (v_1 + v_2).

Note: Arithmetic mean applies ONLY when equal TIME is spent at each speed!
Successive Percentage Change Arithmetic

When a value undergoes two successive percentage changes of +a% and +b%, the net percentage change is a + b + (ab / 100)%.

Note: For a discount of d%, use negative value -d.
Work-Rate Principle Rate Equivalence

If worker A finishes a job in T_A days and B in T_B days, their combined rate is 1/T_A + 1/T_B = 1/T_{total}. Total time = (T_A T_B) / (T_A + T_B).

Note: Pipes & cisterns follow identical rules with negative rates for emptying pipes.
Alligation Mixture Rule Ratios

Ratio of quantity of cheaper ingredient to dearer ingredient in a mixture = (Price of Dearer - Mean Price) / (Mean Price - Price of Cheaper).

Note: Provides fast visual cross-subtraction for weighted averages.
Cube Net Invariant Spatial Aptitude

In any standard 2D net of a 3D cube, alternating faces in a straight row or column form opposite faces and can NEVER be adjacent on the folded cube.

Note: Opposite faces can never be visible simultaneously from any camera perspective.
Quantitative Reasoning Archetypes Quantitative Aptitude
  • Work, Rate & Time: Combined work rate 1/T = sum 1/t_i; Pipes and Cisterns treating outflow as negative work rate.
  • Speed, Distance & Relative Motion: Distance = Speed x Time; Relative speed for opposing directions S_1 + S_2, same direction |S_1 - S_2|.
  • Percentages, Profit & Loss: Profit% = (SP - CP)/CP x 100; successive percentage change formula a + b + (ab/100)%.
  • Combinatorics & Probability: Multiplicative counting rule, Permutations P(n, r), Combinations C(n, r), and classical probability P(E) = n(E)/n(S).
Analytical & Verbal Reasoning Archetypes Reasoning Ability
  • Syllogistic Deductions: Categorical propositions (Universal Affirmative, Particular Negative); evaluated via Venn diagrams or distribution rules.
  • Logical Deduction & Seating Arrangements: Linear and circular sequencing under directional, parity, and neighbor constraints.
  • Blood Relations & Family Trees: Generation-based hierarchical tree representation resolving maternal and paternal linkages.
  • Critical Reasoning & Argument Analysis: Identifying core premise, underlying assumption, argument strengthening/weakening, and valid conclusion.
04 / IN-DEPTH SYLLABUS MODULES

Core Theoretical Modules

Comprehensive subject breakdown containing foundational principles, key theorems, and common examination pitfalls.

MODULE 01

Arithmetic Aptitude, Motion & Work Equivalence

  • Successive percentage change: Two successive changes of +x% and +y% yield net change x + y + (xy / 100)%.
  • Average Speed: For equal distances traveled at speeds v_1 and v_2, Average Speed = 2 v_1 v_2 / (v_1 + v_2) (Harmonic Mean).
  • Work-Rate: If A takes d_A days and B takes d_B days, combined work rate is 1/d_A + 1/d_B = 1/d_{total}.
  • Mixture Alligation: Ratio of Cheaper to Dearer quantity = (Price of Dearer - Mean) / (Mean - Price of Cheaper).
Essential Theorem & Principle Harmonic Average Speed Law: When equal distances are covered at speeds v_1 and v_2, the average speed is given by their Harmonic Mean 2 v_1 v_2 / (v_1 + v_2), NOT their Arithmetic Mean.
⚠️ GATE Exam Trap & Common Mistake Arithmetic Mean Speed Trap: Never average speeds using (v_1 + v_2)/2 unless the body traveled for equal intervals of TIME, not equal distances!
MODULE 02

Logical Deduction, Syllogisms & Seating Arrangements

  • Categorical Syllogisms: Standard forms: All A are B, No A are B, Some A are B, Some A are not B.
  • Euler-Venn diagram method: A conclusion is logically valid iff it holds true across ALL valid Venn diagrams.
  • Blood relations: Always build a generational family tree with explicit gender annotations (+ for male, - for female).
  • Circular seating: Facing center (Right is counter-clockwise, Left is clockwise); facing outward reverses orientations.
Essential Theorem & Principle Syllogistic Deductive Rule: No valid definite conclusion can be drawn from two particular premises (e.g., statements starting with "Some"), nor from two purely negative premises.
⚠️ GATE Exam Trap & Common Mistake "Some" Formal Meaning Trap: In logic, "Some A are B" means "At least one A is B". It does NOT imply that some A are not B; it is entirely compatible with "All A are B".
MODULE 03

Spatial Aptitude, Net Unfolding & Rotational Symmetry

  • Cube Nets: A standard 2D net of a cube unfolds into 6 connected squares. Opposite faces can NEVER share an edge or vertex.
  • Paper Folding: Unfolding punched holes reverses the folds symmetrically across the crease lines.
  • 2D rotations: Rotating a shape 90 degrees clockwise maps coordinate (x, y) to (y, -x).
  • Mirror Reflections: Reflection across vertical axis inverts left-to-right; reflection across horizontal axis inverts top-to-bottom.
Essential Theorem & Principle Cube Net Opposite Face Invariant: In any continuous straight row of 3 or 4 squares in a cube net, alternating squares (separated by exactly one intervening square) form opposite faces on the folded cube.
⚠️ GATE Exam Trap & Common Mistake Simultaneous Opposite Face Fallacy: On any folded 3D cube, two opposite faces can NEVER be visible simultaneously from any camera viewpoint.
05 / CANONICAL COMPARISON MATRIX

Permutations vs. Combinations

Detailed architectural and mathematical comparison of fundamental contrasting concepts frequently tested in GATE:

COMPARISON DIMENSION Permutations (Order Matters) Combinations (Order Irrelevant)
Underlying Concept Arrangement of distinct items where sequence or position is significant. Selection or grouping of items where sequence or order does not matter.
Mathematical Formula P(n, r) = n! / (n - r)!. C(n, r) = n! / (r! * (n - r)!).
Inter-Relationship P(n, r) = r! * C(n, r); Permutations always >= Combinations for r > 1. C(n, r) = P(n, r) / r!; partitions permutations into r! order-equivalent equivalence classes.
Symmetry Invariant P(n, r) != P(n, n - r) (generally distinct values). Pascal Symmetry: C(n, r) == C(n, n - r).
Repetition Variants With repetition allowed: n^r ordered arrangements. With repetition allowed (Stars & Bars): C(n + r - 1, r) unordered selections.
GATE Examination Pitfall Gift exchange among n people where both give: P(n, 2) = n(n-1) directed exchanges. Handshake problem among n people: C(n, 2) = n(n-1)/2 mutual handshakes.
06 / MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION

Combinatorics & Time-Work Equivalence

Quantitative Aptitude 
Permutations & Combinations:
  nPr = n! / (n - r)!   (Ordered arrangement)
  nCr = n! / [ r! * (n - r)! ]   (Unordered selection)

Work-Rate Principle:
  If worker A completes a task in T_A days and B in T_B days:
  Combined Rate = (1 / T_A) + (1 / T_B)
  Total Time T_total = (T_A * T_B) / (T_A + T_B)
